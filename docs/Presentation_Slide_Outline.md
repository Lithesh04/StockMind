# StockMind - Presentation Slide Outline

## Slide 1 - Title
StockMind: An Autonomous Multi-Agent Inventory Monitoring and Supply Chain Optimization System.

## Slide 2 - Problem Statement
Manual stock checks delay shortage detection and supplier coordination. StockMind automates monitoring and replenishment recommendations.

## Slide 3 - Agent Persona and PEAS
Inventory Manager Agent; Performance, Environment, Actuators and Sensors.

## Slide 4 - Architecture
Show the manager agent, reflex reasoner, BDI engine, utility engine, background worker, supplier agent, REST API and security layer.

## Slide 5 - Unit 1 and Unit 2
Condition-action rules, thread/queue monitor and TCP JSON messaging.

## Slide 6 - Unit 3
Buyer-supplier negotiation and 10-offer utility ranking formula.

## Slide 7 - Unit 4
Beliefs, desires, intentions, dynamic re-planning and Open-Meteo integration.

## Slide 8 - Unit 5
Ed25519 signature verification, replay/tamper rejection, Streamlit dashboard and results.

## Slide 9 - Demonstration Results
Show test result (10 passed in the prepared package), measured monitor latency, negotiation result, utility winner and BDI intention switch.

## Slide 10 - Conclusion and Future Work
StockMind demonstrates the full agent lifecycle. Future extensions could add demand forecasting, real ERP integration, persistent supplier accounts and authenticated remote deployment.
